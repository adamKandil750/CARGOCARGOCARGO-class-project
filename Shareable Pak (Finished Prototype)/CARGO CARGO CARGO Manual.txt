CARGO CARGO CARGO Manual

-- INTRODUCTION --
Welcome to CARGO CARGO CARGO! This prototype tasks you with reaching the end of a level in your little truck,
with the aim of delivering your packages. You're given 100 seconds to do so, and you're scored at the end of a
level based on how fast you were able to make it to the finish. To help you out, there are multiple Extensions
to choose from that change the attributes of your truck, but there's a catch. Each Extension will incur a
penalty on your final score, meaning the more Extensions you equip, the more drastic the score reduction will be.
So, you'll have to weigh which Extensions you can and can't take with you, in pursuit of getting the best
score possible. Good luck!

-- CONTROLS --
Below are the controls for this game. Some controls are only accessible when certain Extensions are equipped,
which will be indicated.

MOVE FORWARD/BACKWARD - [W][A]
TURN RIGHT/LEFT - [S][D]
JUMP (Only accessible with Jump Extension) - [SPACEBAR]
BOOST (Only accessible with Boost Extension) - [E]

-- EXTENSIONS --
Below is a brief overview of every single Extension in the game.

Precision Control Extension - Sharpens the control of your truck so it isn't as slippery to manuever.
Armored Front Extension - Adds an armored front plate onto your truck that can bash away obstacles.
Speed Extension - Increases the base speed of your truck.
Boost Extension - Enables the ability to perform a boost with your truck.
Jump Extension - Enables the ability to perform a jump with your truck.
Bounce Extension - Makes your truck bounce against walls.
Invisible Extension - A special extension! This one turns your truck entirely invisible. This is the only
extension that awards to your score instead of taking away from it.
